Overview

What: Move the three featured Medium posts onto vigneshrajsb.com as native, hand-built HTML pages under /writing/<slug>/, with a /writing/ index page and an RSS feed.

Why (product intent): Own the writing on the owned domain: SEO authority accrues to vigneshrajsb.com instead of medium.com, readers get a paywall-free experience that matches the site's hand-built ethos, and the archive stays under Vignesh's control.

Success looks like: All three posts render beautifully in the site's visual language, are indexed by search engines (sitemap, canonical, Article JSON-LD), discoverable by AI agents (llms.txt), and subscribable via /feed.xml. Homepage links point internally.

Plan

Approach, scope, and build sequence. Keep current; refactor the text when the plan changes.

Scope

In: The 3 featured posts (Find My Distributed Trace: AirTags for Datadog APM, Frontend Automation Principles: A Field Guide, Building a Slack Sidekick). A /writing/ index page. Per-post SEO metadata (title, description, canonical, OG/Twitter, Article JSON-LD). Sitemap and llms.txt updates. RSS feed at /feed.xml. Homepage "Notes from the side" links switched to internal URLs.

Out: The other 2 Medium posts (OpenClaw, the intro post). Any changes to the Medium originals, which stay up as-is. Build tooling or static-site generators; everything stays hand-built HTML/CSS. Comments, analytics changes, or a newsletter.

Build sequence

  1. Port content: pull each post's text from Medium, convert to clean semantic HTML (headings, code blocks, images self-hosted under /writing/assets/).
  2. Design the post template and /writing/ index via the delegated design workflow (build subagent with Impeccable, then separate Impeccable review subagent), extending the existing Vollkorn/Karla visual language.
  3. Instantiate the template for all 3 posts with per-post metadata: self-canonical URL, meta description, OG/Twitter tags, Article JSON-LD with author and original publish dates.
  4. Wire discovery: add the 4 new URLs to sitemap.xml with lastmod, list posts in llms.txt, create /feed.xml (RSS 2.0, hand-maintained).
  5. Update the homepage: point "Notes from the side" titles at /writing/<slug>/, keep the "More on Medium" link for the unmigrated archive.
  6. Validate: preview locally, capture screenshots, verify metadata with a structured-data check, confirm feed parses.

Design

Visual direction and design decisions. Filled through the delegated design workflow (build subagent, then Impeccable review subagent).

Direction (planned): Extend the homepage's quiet editorial language: Vollkorn for display and body serifs, Karla for eyebrows and UI text, same measure and spacing rhythm. Post pages should read like essays, not blog-platform pages: generous line length control, restrained code-block styling, minimal chrome (a small back-link to /writing/, title, date, body, footer).

Impeccable review summary: Independent audit passed at 18/20 with no blockers. Should-fixes applied: 44px tap targets on crumb and post-foot links, de-duplicated the archive's first-entry title/blurb, hero alt text now describes the image instead of echoing the figcaption, RSS alternate link added to all writing pages, explicit monospace stack on pre. Nice-to-haves applied: og:image on the archive, deep-link anchors on all field-guide headings, monospace exception documented in DESIGN.md. Open: title-casing differs between homepage entries (sentence case) and post titles (Medium's Title Case) — awaiting author's call.

Notes & callouts

Info

AI crawler access is already open: robots.txt is a wildcard Allow and /llms.txt exists. No repo change needed for bot access.

Watch out

If the site is hosted on Vercel, verify the dashboard Firewall/Bot-protection "Block AI bots" toggle is off — it blocks at the edge regardless of robots.txt.

Watch out

Because the Medium originals stay up and self-canonicalize, Google may keep the Medium copy as the ranking result for some queries. Native pages self-canonicalize too; submitting the sitemap in Search Console after launch gives our copies the best chance.
