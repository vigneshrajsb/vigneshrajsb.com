Ink Tailpiece

Owner: Vignesh  ·  Repo: vigneshrajsb.com  ·  Status: Done  ·  Updated: 2026-08-14

Overview

What: Every page ends with a small ink mark in the text column: Vignesh, Chocolata, and his wife standing on a single brush stroke, a faint sunset stroke on the water behind them. The stroke is the last ink on the page.

Why: The successor to the rolled-back full-bleed pier footer (see its retrospective). The owner's brief that unlocked it: "brush strokes at the end of pages like in an old school note, minimal height, perception of the landscape without being too flat, not cheap." A tailpiece (book-tradition end mark) at column scale instead of a backdrop at full bleed: scale down, quality up. The figures are the family's real silhouettes, thresholded from photos.

Design & build

Composition: one calligraphic pier-edge stroke (pressure swell, dry-brush tail right), the three figures standing on it left of center, backs implied toward the lake, one warm two-stroke sunset whisper behind them (thicker low, fainter high, the near-wide/far-thin depth cue the owner identified). Vollkorn-ink monochrome plus the single peach accent.

Figures: thresholded from family photos (owner-provided), feet flattened to a shared baseline, smoothed to brush weight, tinted the site ink. Height anchored to reality: Vignesh 6ft, his wife 5ft (83%), Chocolata at 62%. Order: me · dog · her.

Asset: one self-contained 16.7 KB SVG (assets/tailpiece.svg): stroke paths with an feTurbulence roughen, silhouettes embedded at 3x as data URIs. Transparent background, so no color-matching fragility. In-flow figure.tailpiece inside main on all 6 pages, so short pages end naturally (the failure that helped kill the footer).

Owner-tuned spec (via the live slider tuner, final):

human 54px · wife 83% · dog 62% · gap 8px · pair at 34% · sink 0
stroke: 100% span · 0.65 weight | stage 120px · 8rem above · 0 below
sunset: 36% x · 232px wide · 30px up · 0.5 opacity | order me·dog·her

"0 below" means the tailpiece's negative bottom margin cancels main's 6rem padding: the stroke is the final pixel row of every page.
