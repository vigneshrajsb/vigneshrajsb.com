Pronounce Helper

Owner: Vignesh  ·  Repo: vigneshrajsb.com  ·  Status: Done  ·  Updated: 2026-08-14

Overview

What: A small "▸ wig-neysh" button beside the masthead name that plays a short audio clip of the name's pronunciation.

Why: Most visitors guess the name wrong; the helper answers the question before it is asked, in text for skimmers and in audio for anyone who clicks. It sits on the baseline where Chocolata used to keep the name company.

Plan & design

Decisions (owner interview, 2026-08-14): text plus audio; TTS-generated clip; friendly respelling written exactly as the owner spelled it, "wig-neysh" (w sound, "ey" as in hey); placed beside the name.

Audio: five candidates generated locally with macOS speech synthesis (Samantha and Rishi voices, native and phonetic spellings); owner picked Rishi saying "Vignesh" natively as the most natural. Ships as assets/pronounce-vignesh.m4a (7.6 KB AAC).

Mechanics: a real <button> in the nameplate reading /wig‑neysh/ in Vollkorn italic at tertiary ink: dictionary slashes carry the "this is how you say it" convention with no underline or icon competing with the name. Hover deepens the ink one shade as the click affordance. assets/pronounce.js lazily creates the Audio object on first click, so nothing loads until asked. Tap target padded to 44px; aria-label says what the button does; homepage only.

Design iteration: v1 used Karla with a dotted underline and play triangle; the owner flagged the underline as pulling the gaze off the name. Six treatments were compared in situ; the owner picked the serif italic slashes (quietest editorial blend), per the recommendation.
